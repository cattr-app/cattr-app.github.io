What is Cattr?

Cattr is a open source time tracking solution, designed to be flawlessly integrated in your infrastructure. Superpowered with built-in screenshot capture and activity detection, it's a great instrument to keep your team's perfomance on top.

Our Benefits

Open Source

Community version is published under SSPL license, so you can use it for free. Forever.

Track you activity

Built-in inactivity detection and screenshot capturing helps you to focus on
the work.

On-premises

Your private data is kept inside your own infrastructure, so no more pain with data protection regulations.

Extensions

Extend basic functionality with the community plugins, or even write your own one!
